Privacy Policy

This policy describes the personal ClipWatch desktop installation and its use of Google and YouTube services.

Information and purposes

The application reads public video identifiers, titles, channel information, dates, durations and viewing statistics to organize video information. With the operator's authorization, it reads the selected channel's identifier, name and upload list to confirm the destination and avoid duplicate uploads. It uploads video files, titles, descriptions, credits and visibility settings to that channel.

It does not request access to Gmail, contacts, Google Drive files or Google passwords.

Storage and security

API credentials and OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in the operator's local installation. The local database stores video information, observations and job records. Media and review files are also stored locally. Credentials and upload session URLs are omitted from application error logs. The operator is responsible for protecting the device and backups.

Services and sharing

Requests needed for authorization, reading and uploading are sent to Google. Media retrieval connects to the relevant media providers. Scheduled editorial review in Codex uses an external AI service to inspect candidate metadata, frames and captions. Google credentials and passwords are not supplied to the model for that review. Video composition runs locally.

Uploaded videos and metadata are subject to the chosen visibility and YouTube's rules. Public uploads can be viewed and shared by others. Google handles information under its Privacy Policy. The installation does not include data sales or personalized advertising.

Retention and deletion

Maintenance normally removes old candidate observations and eligible published media after seven days. Performance observations for the operator's uploads may be retained for up to 90 days. Other metadata, job records, review packages, support media and credentials may remain until the operator deletes them. These periods describe local maintenance, not deletion from external services.

To delete local data, stop the application and remove the relevant files and backups. Removing local files does not delete uploaded videos; manage those in YouTube Studio.

Website

These static pages have no login forms, analytics scripts or advertising. They are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process connection information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Revoke access

The operator can revoke access through Google Account connections and delete the locally saved token. Further authorized actions require reconnecting.

Google user data

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

Operator and contact

The operator is the owner of this personal installation and its associated Google Cloud project. The support contact is displayed on the application's Google authorization screen.
