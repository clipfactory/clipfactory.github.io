ClipFactory

ClipWatch is a personal desktop application for reviewing public video information and uploading videos to a YouTube channel authorized by its operator.

Google authorization is used to identify the selected channel, read its upload list and upload videos. Access can be revoked in the operator's Google Account.

This website provides application information and policies. It does not collect passwords or access tokens.
